# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese cross-border e-commerce sellers and brand owners who need to create product listings for multiple international platforms (Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, AliExpress). They work in a browser on desktop, often with multiple tabs open for research. Their primary task is generating and managing AI-powered product copy and marketing images efficiently. They value speed, professional output quality, and platform-specific optimization.

## Product Purpose

AI跨境通 (AI Cross-border E-commerce Assistant) generates multi-platform product copy and marketing images from a single product brief. It supports Chinese/English/Spanish (plus German, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Arabic) and helps Chinese brands expand overseas efficiently. Success means a seller can input product details once and get platform-optimized listings across Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop and more.

## Brand Personality

Professional, capable, trustworthy. Speaks with expertise but not arrogance. The tone is efficient and helpful, like a skilled operations consultant. Three words: **sharp, reliable, empowering**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with excessive whitespace and low information density
- "AI startup" clichés: purple gradients, floating geometric shapes, overly playful illustrations
- Enterprise tools that feel heavy, slow, or bureaucratic
- The hero-metric template (big number + small label) used without context
- Dark mode as a default "because tools look cool dark"

## Design Principles

1. **Show progress, not promises.** Every action produces visible results. Empty states guide action; loading states show meaningful progress.
2. **Reduce friction, increase confidence.** Minimize clicks for common tasks. Surface smart defaults and AI suggestions proactively.
3. **One product, many platforms.** Platform-specific optimization is the core value proposition. Make platform choice visible and consequential.
4. **Professional polish, not decorative fluff.** Visual quality serves clarity. Animation, color, and spacing have purpose, not just decoration.
5. **Work at the speed of thought.** Batch operations, templates, and keyboard-friendly flows for power users who manage many products.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- System font stack (already implemented, no Google Fonts dependency for network-restricted users)
- Dark and light mode with system preference detection
- Support for reduced motion preferences
